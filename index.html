What I do

OCP Maintenance SolutionsJorf Lasfar · 2025–2026

Flow-to-level override on a scrubber valve

On a gas scrubber, a valve controls the flow of washing water. This water then goes to tank 07, which feeds several pumps and process users.

Sometimes the valve that fills tank 07 is open at 100% and the level still drops. I wrote a program for this case: the scrubber valve stops following its flow setpoint and follows the level of tank 07.

Press “Increase demand” and watch the two valves.
